# ISL Non-Manual Marker Taxonomy Specification (Prompt 66)

## 1. Grounded Linguistic Taxonomy (7 Categories)

### Category: `eyebrow_movement`

- **`eyebrows_furrowed`**: Furrowed eyebrows for WH-questions (where, when, who, why)
  - *Linguistic Source:* Zeshan (2004) Hand shape and Non-manuals in ISL Syntax
- **`eyebrows_raised`**: Raised eyebrows for Topic marking and Yes/No questions
  - *Linguistic Source:* Vasishta et al. (1985) An Introduction to ISL

### Category: `head_movement`

- **`head_nod_slight`**: Slight forward head nod for affirmative declarative statements
  - *Linguistic Source:* ISLRTC Technical Guidelines (2020) Non-manual Grammar
- **`head_shake_negation`**: Side-to-side headshake co-occurring with negative signs/predicates
  - *Linguistic Source:* Zeshan (2004) Negation in Indian Sign Language
- **`head_tilt`**: Head tilt indicating inquiry or spatial referent orientation
  - *Linguistic Source:* ISLRTC Guidelines (2020)

### Category: `facial_expression`

- **`eyes_wide_expressive`**: Wide open eyes for emergency or high urgency signals
  - *Linguistic Source:* Vasishta et al. (1985)
- **`squint_intense`**: Squinting for intense focus or symptom severity description
  - *Linguistic Source:* Zeshan (2004)
- **`neutral_facial`**: Neutral facial posture for baseline declarative turns
  - *Linguistic Source:* ISLRTC Guidelines (2020)

### Category: `mouth_pattern`

- **`mouth_open_ah`**: Open mouth 'ah' shape for intensity or spatial scope
  - *Linguistic Source:* ISLRTC Technical Guidelines (2020)
- **`mouth_closed_flat`**: Flat closed mouth posture
  - *Linguistic Source:* ISLRTC Guidelines (2020)
- **`mouth_mouthing_gloss`**: Silent English word mouthing for technical/medical terms
  - *Linguistic Source:* Zeshan (2004) Mouthing in ISL

### Category: `body_lean`

- **`body_lean_forward`**: Forward upper body lean for question engagement
  - *Linguistic Source:* Zeshan (2004)
- **`body_lean_back`**: Backward body lean for contrastive topic or hesitation
  - *Linguistic Source:* Vasishta et al. (1985)

### Category: `question_markers`

- **`q_marker_wh`**: Question marker for content questions (WH)
  - *Linguistic Source:* Zeshan (2004) ISL Interrogatives
- **`q_marker_yn`**: Question marker for polar confirmation questions (Yes/No)
  - *Linguistic Source:* ISLRTC Guidelines (2020)

### Category: `negation`

- **`neg_marker_headshake`**: Explicit negation marker combining headshake and NO gloss
  - *Linguistic Source:* Zeshan (2004)
- **`neg_marker_absence`**: Absence negation marker for non-existent symptom/item
  - *Linguistic Source:* ISLRTC Guidelines (2020)
